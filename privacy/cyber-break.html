Privacy Policy – Cyber Break

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Cyber Break ("the App", package com.nplabs.cyberbreak) is a single-player text adventure game developed by NP LABS ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Short version

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The App works entirely offline on your device.

Information we collect

None. Cyber Break does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or any third-party services.

Internet and permissions

The App does not request the Internet permission and does not connect to any network. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive device feature.

Local data

Your game progress and settings (for example saved games and language preference) are stored only on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third parties

The App contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or external services. No data is shared with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected in this document.
