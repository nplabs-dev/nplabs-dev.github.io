Privacy Policy – Protoplast Card Game

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Protoplast Card Game ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), package name com.nplabs.protoplastcardgame, handles information when you use it. By installing and using the App you agree to the practices described below. Contact: dtdvnp@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

The App never asks for your real name, email address, phone number or payment details. To offer ranked online play, online save and leaderboards, the App uses a game server run by NP LABS on Cloudflare. The App does not show ads.

  • Online player account. When you go online, the App creates a game account on our server with the player name you choose, your allegiance (Silicon or Bio) and the portrait you pick. Player names are public: other players see them in matches and on the leaderboard. The account is identified by a random key stored on your device; the server keeps only a hash of that key.
  • Google Play Games. If your phone is signed in to Google Play Games, the App links your game account to your Play Games player ID, so you can recover it on another device. We receive your Play Games player ID and the display name of your Play Games profile; we do not receive your email address or your Google password.
  • Online save. Your custom decks, card collection, in-game currencies, packs, cosmetics, level, statistics and tutorial progress are saved on our server, so they are not lost if you change phone or reinstall the App.
  • Ranked matches. For each online match we store the two players, the decks used, the result, the rank changes and a record of the moves (used to settle disputed results), with the date and time.
  • Technical data. Like any internet service, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the service and protect it from abuse. We do not use it to identify you or build a profile.
  • Card downloads. Card art and game data are downloaded from our server as plain files; no personal data is sent to download them.
  • In-app purchases (Google Play Billing). Purchases of Matter Crystals are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment details; Google handles all payment information. The App receives a purchase confirmation to deliver what you bought.
  • Local Wi-Fi multiplayer. When you play against other people on the same local Wi-Fi network, the App exchanges your player name and the game moves directly between the devices on that network. This data is not sent to our server.
  • Local data. Settings, profiles and game progress are also stored locally on your device.

2. How we use information

  • run online matches, matchmaking by rank and the leaderboard;
  • save your progress online and restore it on a new device;
  • keep player names unique and prevent impersonation;
  • deliver purchased content;
  • keep the App working correctly, fix problems and balance the game.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare (game server hosting) — cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google Play Games Services — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data retention and deleting your account

We keep your online account and online save for as long as the account exists. You can delete it at any time inside the App: Edit Profile → Delete Operator. Deleting removes your player name, your Google Play Games link, your device keys and your online save, and takes you off the leaderboard. Past match records are kept only in anonymised form, so the other player's history stays complete. If you no longer have the App, request deletion at protoplast-api.nplabs.workers.dev/delete-account or by email.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. Contact us at dtdvnp@gmail.com for any request about your data. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App.

6. Security

All connections between the App and our server are encrypted (HTTPS). Account keys are stored on the server only as hashes.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
